Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026

Global Gates ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Pray for the Cup app (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information when you use our Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you. This may include your name and email address. We use this information to save your prayer progress across devices and to send devotional email updates (registered users only).

Prayer Activity Data

If you create an account, your prayer activity — nations prayed, devotionals completed, streaks, and team data — is stored securely in our cloud database (Firebase/Google). If you use the app without registering, all data remains on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

Log Data

We collect standard log data that your browser or device sends when you use our Service. This may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of your visit, and other statistics.

Cookies

We use cookies and local browser storage to save your app progress and preferences. You can clear this data at any time through your browser or device settings. Note that clearing this data will reset your local prayer progress if you are not registered.

Service Providers

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Firebase (Google) — secure cloud database and authentication · firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Brevo — email delivery for devotional updates · brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy
  • Vercel — app hosting and deployment · vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

These providers have access to your Personal Information only to perform tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

Security

The security of your Personal Information is important to us. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with Personal Information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it immediately.

Compliance With Laws

We will disclose your Personal Information where required to do so by law or subpoena.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at info@globalgates.info or visit globalgates.info/contact.
